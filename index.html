Top 5 Roblox Games

#1 – Brookhaven 🏡

A life-simulation game where you roleplay and live your dream virtual life.

Rating: ★★★★★

#2 – Blox Fruits ⚔️

Battle, level up, and explore islands with special abilities based on the One Piece anime.

Raise pets, decorate homes, and explore a magical world with friends.

Rating: ★★★★☆

#4 – Doors

A thrilling horror puzzle experience with procedurally generated rooms and jump scares.

Rating: ★★★★☆

#5 – BedWars

A fast-paced PvP game where you protect your bed and destroy others'.

Rating: ★★★★☆
